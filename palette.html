Palette

Materials

Materials are used to emulate natural interfaces that reflect nature. Paper can be used to isolate context, content or stand out over backgrounds to show clear intent for the user.

Glass can be used to show content behind and provide the same intent that paper does with a more natural blend to the content behind.
